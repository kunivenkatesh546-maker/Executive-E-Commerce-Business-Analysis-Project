# 🛍️ Executive Business Analysis: Ecommerce Conversion Funnel Optimization


> A complete end-to-end business analytics case study using SQL, Python, and Power BI to identify customer drop-offs, analyze marketing performance, and provide actionable recommendations for improving ecommerce conversions.

---

# 📖 Project Overview

An ecommerce business observed fluctuations in customer conversion rates and wanted to understand why users were dropping off before completing purchases.

This project simulates the role of a Business Analyst responsible for transforming raw Google Analytics 4 data into business insights that leadership can use to improve customer acquisition, conversion, and revenue.

The analysis focuses on answering critical business questions through SQL, Python, and interactive dashboards.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze the customer purchase funnel
- Identify the largest conversion bottlenecks
- Measure funnel conversion rates
- Perform root cause analysis
- Evaluate traffic source performance
- Analyze user behavior across devices and countries
- Identify top-performing products
- Recommend business actions to improve conversion and revenue

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- The largest customer drop-off occurs before users view products.
- Google is the highest-performing traffic acquisition channel.
- Desktop users generate the majority of website traffic.
- Premium-priced products generate higher revenue despite lower purchase volumes.
- Checkout completion is relatively strong compared to earlier funnel stages.

---

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

### Improve Product Discovery

Optimize homepage layout and product recommendations to encourage more users to view products.

### Optimize Product Pages

Improve product descriptions, images, reviews, and pricing transparency.

### Increase Marketing Efficiency

Invest more in high-performing acquisition channels while reviewing low-performing campaigns.

### Focus on High-Value Products

Promote products generating the highest revenue through featured collections and targeted campaigns.
